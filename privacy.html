Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Who we are

This site is operated by Carter's Computer Services ("we", "us"), which also publishes the Level Up Society and Hotboistep Gaming brands. You can reach us through the contact form on the home page.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us:

  • The name, email address, service and message you enter in our contact or enrolment forms.
  • For trading academy enquiries, the name and email address you submit before being redirected to our payment provider.

We do not use tracking or advertising cookies, and we do not build profiles of visitors.

How we use it

Your details are used for one purpose only: to reply to your enquiry and, if you go on to book, to deliver the service. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not add you to a mailing list unless you ask us to.

Who else sees it

Form submissions are delivered to us as a message through Telegram, which stores that message on its own servers under its privacy policy. Where a form redirects you to a payment page, that payment is handled by our checkout provider and their terms apply to it.

Our site is hosted on Vercel, which records standard technical request logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability.

How long we keep it

Enquiry messages are kept only as long as needed to deal with your request and any follow-up work, and are deleted on request. Ask us at any time and we will remove your details.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Send the request through the contact form and we will respond within one business day. If you are in the UK or EU, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Affiliate links

Some outbound links on this site are affiliate links — in particular Amazon (as an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases) and Magica. If you buy through one of those links we may receive a small commission at no extra cost to you. These links are clearly labelled where they appear, and they never change the price you pay.

Children

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page.
